# Kontainer

> User-friendly DAM & PIM for companies to organize all digital assets like product data, images, videos, or size guides in a professional way.

Every page is also served as Markdown: append `.md` to its URL. The front page is https://kontainer.com/index.md.

Translations live under a language prefix (Dansk: https://kontainer.com/da, Deutsch: https://kontainer.com/de, Español: https://kontainer.com/es, Nederlands: https://kontainer.com/nl, Svenska: https://kontainer.com/sv) and are listed with their hreflang in https://kontainer.com/sitemap.xml.
